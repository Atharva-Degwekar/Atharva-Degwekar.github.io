PhonePe Smart Spend Coach

PhonePe shows users what they spent but never what to change. I took Smart Spend Coach from an unvalidated idea to a business case leadership could greenlight.

UsersTwo personas: Convenience-First Priya (tier-1 professional, ~15–20M users) and Budget-Building Rohit (tier-2 family, ~10–12M).
ChoiceRICE-scored 4 ideas. A real-time, one-tap nudge inside the payment flow won at 4.0, ahead of a weekly email digest at 3.0.
SignalOf 9,520 users who viewed an insight, 70% didn't act. Usability testing traced this to a “Set Cap” button sitting below the fold.
  • Designed a 6-screen Figma prototype with a reusable NudgeCard component, including the “not enough data” edge case.
  • Designed a 14-day A/B test (2,000 users per variant) with action-rate, time-to-action and dismiss-rate guardrails.
  • Set freemium decoy pricing (₹0 / ₹79 / ₹99), a referral loop and a 5-3-1 KPI dashboard.

Kavach: foreign tourist safety

India welcomed 9.5M+ foreign tourists in 2023, but no platform combines foreigner identity, embassy escalation and crowd-risk prediction. Kavach is the coordination layer that routes a traveller's SOS to the right real-world responder.

GapChecked 6 existing systems, including 112 ERSS, e-FRRO, DigiYatra and Amarnath RFID. None of them covers all three needs.
BuildThree connected surfaces sharing one data model: a traveller SOS app, an operator console and an admin console.
TrustOperators see only location and NFC ID during an alert. Passport data stays with immigration, in line with the DPDP Act.
  • Designed hold-to-confirm SOS, nearest-unit dispatch by real distance, auto-flagged female-responder and embassy rules, and RBAC with confirmed sensitive scopes.
  • Wrote the GTM: B2G licensing, a Jaipur and Kochi pilot, then a phased rollout to 15+ cities, with the border corridor run as an MHA-led track.
  • Audited every market figure against primary sources and replaced a weak blog statistic with NCRB data: 238 cases in 2023.

ModelFreemium: Free with 5 mocks a month, Pro at ₹499 and Premium at ₹699, plus a yearly plan at a 17% discount.

RailIQ: train location you can verify

High-speed rail operators rely on trackside systems to know where a train is. RailIQ adds an independent check using mobile-network signals, which helps in incidents and reduces false alarms.

SpecA product specification with six modules and a traceable FR code on every requirement, written ahead of a technical session with the client and its systems integrator.
ConsoleA clickable fleet console: live map, incidents, recurring patterns, staff and anchors, emergency broadcast, and a per-coach incident drill-down.
ScopeI set a clear boundary. False-positive reduction is in scope; preventing incidents is the signalling system's job.
  • Designed a tamper-evident audit trail with two access tiers: operational, and investigative access that needs judicial authorisation.
  • Tagged every console page with its FR number so stakeholders could trace each requirement to the UI.
